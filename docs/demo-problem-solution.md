# EIF Compass problem and solution demo

Target length: about four minutes. Use approved demo records and a frozen deployment. Show the problem and the resulting support decision; keep navigation focused on one intern and one lead.

## Opening problem

“An intern’s tasks, requirements and feedback are spread across channels. They spend time asking what is due and what to do next. Leads repeat the same reminders and chase progress updates.”

## Intern solution

Open the intern dashboard. Point to the greeting, current or next milestone, requirement status and suggested next action. Say: “This brings the next step and its deadline into one place.” Open Explain this suggestion. The assistant explains the same rule-based choice using permitted records; it does not grade the intern. If AI is unavailable, return to the dashboard and show the recommendation remains usable.

## Lead solution

Switch to the lead workspace. Show the team progress and one open blocker, then its discussion or resolution control. Say: “The lead can see where support is needed without asking everyone for another status update.” Avoid changing live records unless they are dedicated demo records.

## Close

“EIF Compass centralizes fellowship work so interns can identify their next action and leads can focus on mentoring.” Describe reduced tracking time and clearer priorities as intended benefits, not measured results. State that people retain control over decisions and that AI cannot grade, rank or decide pass/fail.

## Prepare before the presentation

Choose one representative incomplete task with a real upcoming or overdue deadline, one team milestone in an active sprint, one requirement and one blocker. Confirm the intended intern and lead accounts see those records. Use the configured stable deployment after the dependent PRs merge. Have screenshots and a short backup recording available. Do not run the local-only synthetic Auth seed on the hosted database.

Keep Calendar OAuth, file management, every task filter and all settings outside the main story unless the audience asks. They can be supporting capabilities without becoming a full-system tour.
